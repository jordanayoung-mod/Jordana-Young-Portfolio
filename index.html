Microsoft, Copilot Studio

Defined the target user and MVP for the Copilot Studio Operate Hub, a new product

Question
What needs is this platform not addressing?
Method
Think-aloud interviews with 18 Admins and operators in 4 countries, plus telemetry analysis.
Insight
Operators who run agents they didn't build, need visibility into quality, drift, cost and risk in one centralized place.
Research recommendation
13 operator needs prioritized into the MVP and later stages.
Impact
Defined the target user and MVP scope; evaluation built in as a core tab.

How do builders know an agent is good enough to ship?

35 interviews with users and non-users; Quantitative validation, N=4000

Meta, Instagram Shops

Shaped a personalized product page and deprioritized a planned feature that wouldn't drive sales

Question
How do we optimize the shopping experience across categories to drive revenue?
Method
12 interviews, then multivariate discrete choice study with N= 9,000 shoppers.
Insight
9 of 17 product details drove purchases, and the price guarantee wasn't one of them. 6 cross-category details drive conversion.
Research recommendation
Prioritize 9 variables, deprioritize one planned variable that would not drive sales.
Impact
Variables trained the model behind a personalized product page; non-performing variable dropped.
  • In-depth interviews
  • Multivariate discrete choice

Meta, Express Wi-Fi

Turned around a failing Wi-Fi rollout in Delhi

Question
Why was Delhi underperforming when other markets succeeded? Telemetry data yielded no clues.
Method
Interviews, usage data by gender, and access point mapping with satellite imagery overlay.
Insight
Sign-up demanded literacy levels and ID types many people didn't have, and women couldn't safely use street hotspots.
Research recommendation
Adapt Wi-Fi frequency to enable in-home Wi-Fi use in Delhi's narrow street layout; drastically simplify the sign-in page, do not require ID, and optimize for low literacy.
Impact
+36% weekly registrations after the fixes.
  • In-depth interviews
  • Ethnography
  • Cross-cultural research
  • Usage-data analysis
  • Access-point mapping

SodaStream, PepsiCo

Reversed a churn problem with patented hardware and new factories

Question
What was really driving churn?
Method
Multi-market in-home ethnography.
Insight

Users ran out of gas before establishing a seltzer habit.

Having only 1 bottle, rather than 2 to 3 chilling, also made that habit harder to build.

Some unknowingly bought cylinders that weren't genuine SodaStream.

Research recommendation
Determined need to provide additional bottles and cylinders in starter kit, as well as more recognizable cylinder branding and patented value hardware
Impact
Patented quick-connect cylinder and valve, a new machine, wider home delivery, and revamped starter kit configuration. Tooling of 2 new factories to enable this.
  • Multi-market in-home ethnography
